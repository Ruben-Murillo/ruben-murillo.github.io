Arduino RC Car

My first mechatronics project: a 3D-printed, Bluetooth-controlled vehicle integrating mechanical and electrical design.

ArduinoBluetoothMotor DriverCAD3D Printing

Project Overview

Designed and fabricated a 3D-printed RC platform controlled by an Arduino and motor driver. The vehicle receives Bluetooth commands from a smartphone.

Key Features

  • Arduino embedded control.
  • Bluetooth command interface.
  • Motor-driver integration.
  • 3D-printed mechanical platform.
  • Circuit schematic and CAD/isometric documentation.

The finished vehicle demonstrated responsive control at distances of roughly 30 ft.
